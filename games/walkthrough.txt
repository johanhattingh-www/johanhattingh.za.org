THE HOBBIT - OFFICIAL WALKTHROUGH & SOLUTION GUIDE

Welcome, adventurous burglar! To complete your epic journey from Bag End to Smaug's Lair, follow these steps precisely:

1. BAG END & HOBBITON
   - LOOK
   - TAKE RING
   - TAKE LETTER
   - EAST (to hallway)
   - TAKE WALKING STICK
   - EAST (to larder)
   - TAKE PIE
   - WEST
   - WEST (back to Bag End)
   - OUT (to Hobbiton)
   - TALK THORIN (or ASK THORIN ABOUT KEY)
   - TAKE MAP

2. THE ROAD & TROLLSHAWS
   - NORTH (to Dusty Road)
   - EAST (to Trollshaws)
   - TAKE KEY
   - TAKE PURSE

3. RIVENDELL & THE MOUNTAINS
   - EAST (to Rivendell)
   - TAKE BLADE
   - TAKE SHIELD
   - EAST (to Misty Mountains)
   - DOWN (into Goblin Tunnels)

4. GOLLUM'S CAVE & MIRKWOOD
   - TAKE RING (if not already taken)
   - EAST (to Mirkwood)
   - TAKE BOW
   - EAST (to Lake-town)
   - TAKE SPEAR

5. EREBOR & SMAUG'S LAIR
   - NORTH (to Lonely Mountain)
   - TAKE ARKENSTONE
   - ENTER (into Smaug's Lair)
   - TAKE GOLDEN CUP

VICTORY!
You have successfully looted Smaug's hoard and completed your quest. Return to your armchair at Bag End as a wealthy and legendary hobbit!
